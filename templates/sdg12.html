🌍 Sustainable Development Goal 12

Responsible Consumption and Production

SDG 12 aims to ensure sustainable consumption and production patterns. It encourages people to reduce waste, conserve natural resources, and adopt environmentally friendly practices.

Objectives

  • 💧 Save Water
  • ⚡ Reduce Electricity Consumption
  • 🍽️ Reduce Food Waste
  • ♻️ Encourage Recycling
  • 🌍 Protect Natural Resources

Our Contribution

The "Save Today Sustain Tomorrow" website helps users monitor water usage, electricity consumption, and food waste while encouraging sustainable habits through a sustainability score.
